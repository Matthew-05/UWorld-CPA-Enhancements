## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-22

Initial release.

### Added

- Peer-average score marker for the test-results *Points Scored* bar, comparing your score with
  the mean of the `% CORRECT OTHERS` column.
- Result-colored question review navigation for Tutor mode: green (correct), orange (incorrect)
  and gray (unanswered).
- Restored `Ctrl/Cmd+F`, `F3`, `Ctrl/Cmd+G`, text selection, copy and right-click on UWorld.
- Popup with a per-feature toggle for each modification, stored in `chrome.storage.sync`, plus a
  refresh button.
